# Paid Media Plan — Marketplace Traffic & Conversion (FY 2026)

**Objective:** Drive qualified, high-intent traffic to **Amazon & Flipkart** product listings to improve discovery, sales intent, and conversions.
**Strategic levers:** Traffic quality > raw volume · Always-on retargeting · Seasonal demand capture · Festive purchase intent.
**Total paid media budget:** **₹30,00,000 (₹30L)**
**Planning horizon:** End-June 2026 → mid-January 2027 (+ new-season ramp from mid-Jan).

> All estimates below are planning benchmarks for Indian retail/consumer-durables inventory (2026). Actuals to be trued-up after the first 2 weeks of live data and reallocated toward the best-performing platform/audience.

---

## 1. Platform-wise Budget Bifurcation

### Recommended split (revised from the suggested starting point)

| Platform | Suggested Start | **Recommended** | Budget | Δ vs. start |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta (FB + Instagram) | 45% | **50%** | **₹15.0L** | +5% |
| Google Display Network (GDN) | 25% | **25%** | **₹7.5L** | — |
| Programmatic Display | 30% | **25%** | **₹7.5L** | −5% |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100%** | **₹30.0L** | |

### Rationale

- **Meta → 50% (up from 45%).** Best engine for *qualified* marketplace traffic. Granular interest + lookalike + in-market layering, the strongest retargeting stack (DPA / Advantage+ Catalog), and the lowest cost per quality landing-page view of the three. Carries the bulk of sales-intent and festive purchase-intent demand. Instagram in particular indexes well for appliance discovery among 25–44 urban audiences.
- **GDN → 25% (held).** Lowest CPC of the three → cheapest incremental reach and the most cost-efficient retargeting via display remarketing + Custom Intent. Excellent for keeping the brand present across the long consideration cycle of high-ticket appliances. Held flat as an efficient prospecting + retargeting workhorse.
- **Programmatic → 25% (down from 30%).** Programmatic display delivers premium/contextual inventory and cross-device reach but carries the **highest effective CPC and CPM** of the three, so it is the least efficient on a pure *qualified-click* basis. Trimmed 5% and redeployed to Meta. Retained at a meaningful 25% for premium festive inventory, contextual relevance (e.g., weather/AQI-triggered), and DV360 cross-device retargeting that Meta/GDN can't fully cover.

> If marketplace-side conversion signal (Amazon Attribution / Flipkart) confirms programmatic is driving incremental add-to-carts during festive, scale it back up at the expense of GDN prospecting.

## 3. Audience Targeting Approach

**Prospecting (top + mid funnel)**
- **In-market segments:** Air Conditioners, Refrigerators, Washing Machines, Air Purifiers, Home Appliances (Google/DV360).
- **Custom Intent / Custom Audiences:** category + competitor keywords, "buy [product] Amazon/Flipkart", model/spec searches.
- **Interest & demographic (Meta):** home appliances, home improvement, new movers, new parents, premium-electronics affinity; 25–54, urban Metro + Tier-1/2.
- **Lookalikes (Meta) / Similar (DV360):** 1–3% from site visitors, add-to-cart, video viewers, and past purchasers.
- **Seasonal/contextual layering:**
  - Monsoon → humidity/dehumidifier + laundry intent.
  - Air Purifier → **AQI/pollution-triggered** contextual + Delhi-NCR & North India weighting in Nov–Jan.
  - AC/Refrigerator → temperature-triggered + hot-region weighting from mid-Jan.

**Retargeting (lower funnel) — always on**
- Site visitors tiered **7 / 30 / 60 / 90-day**; product-page viewers; cart abandoners; video viewers (50%/75%); ad & page engagers.
- **Dynamic Product Ads** serving the exact viewed SKU with the live marketplace price/offer.
- Cross-device retargeting via DV360 to recover users who researched on desktop and buy on mobile (typical for high-ticket appliances).
- Frequency caps to protect quality: ~3–4/week prospecting, up to 6–8/week retargeting during sale/festive.

**Geo weighting:** Pan-India base, with category-seasonal overlays (Air Purifier → North; AC → North/West/South heat belts; festive → Metro + Tier-1/2 across the board).

**Phasing logic:** Festive (Diwali) is the single largest demand spike → 30%. Prime Day and Independence Day are short, high-conversion bursts (efficient CPCs, strong marketplace deal traffic). ## 6. Retargeting Approach (always-on backbone)

1. **Audience pool build (Phases 1–3):** push prospecting + launch reach to populate site-visitor, viewer, and engager pools ahead of the big sale windows.
2. **Sequenced messaging:** Awareness creative → feature/benefit consideration → DPA + live marketplace offer (price, EMI, exchange) at the bottom.
3. **Dynamic Product Ads** across Meta + GDN + DV360, served by viewed SKU with the current Amazon/Flipkart price/offer feed.
4. **Recency tiering:** 7-day (hottest, highest bid + offer-led) → 30/60/90-day (re-engage, broader value message).
5. **Cross-device recovery** via DV360 — critical for high-ticket appliances researched on desktop, purchased on mobile.
6. **Spend weighting:** retargeting ≈ **30% of budget always-on**, scaling to **~45%** during Prime Day / Independence Day / Diwali when the pool is largest and intent peaks.
7. **Quality guardrails:** frequency caps, exclusion of recent purchasers/converters, suppression of bounced single-second sessions from lookalike seeds.

## 7. Measurement & Optimization

- **North-star:** qualified sessions to marketplace + assisted marketplace conversions (Amazon Attribution / Flipkart Ads where available).
- **Quality KPIs:** landing-page view rate, bounce/quick-back rate, cost per quality session, retargeting ROAS proxy.
- **Cadence:** weekly optimization; **2-week true-up** to reallocate toward the best platform/audience/SKU; full review after each major phase.
- **Reallocation rule:** shift up to 15% of a phase's budget to the top-performing platform mid-flight without re-approval.
